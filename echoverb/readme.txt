Delay section
----------------
Knob 1 - delay
Knob 2 - feedback
Knob 3 - mix

Reverb section
-----------------
Knob 4 - Reverb Time (Small room to near-infinite)
Knob 5 - Reverb Damping (Internal Cutoff filter from about 500Hz to 20kHz)
Knob 6 - Send Amount (Controls amount of dry signal sent to reverb)

Switch 1 - effect order - off (reverb -> echo), on (echo -> reverb)
Footswitch 1 - on / off for delay
Footswitch 2 - on / off for reverb.
